About The Study Cup

A small café run by students, for students, with one simple idea: a good cup of coffee makes a long day shorter.

Our story

The Study Cup started as a late-night coffee cart outside the library. Students kept asking for somewhere warm to sit while they finished group work, so the cart grew into a real café with real tables.

Today we serve hundreds of cups a week. We still keep the same rules: friendly service, honest food, and a table for anyone who needs one.
